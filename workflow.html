How it is intended to work

The planned workflow keeps the decision and message sending with the person.

  1. Find a conversation. Identify sent B2B proposals that have not received a reply and may need attention.
  2. Review what happened. Let the person decide whether to follow up now, later, or not at all.
  3. Prepare a draft. Create text for the person to review and send themselves, if they choose.

The agent has not been built. These steps describe an intention, not available functionality.
