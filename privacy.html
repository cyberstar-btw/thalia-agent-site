Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Who operates this website

Thalia Agent is an early-stage project operated by an independent developer using the public name cyberstar-btw, based in Switzerland. You can contact the operator at cybersec-star@proton.me.

This notice applies only to the public Thalia Agent website. It does not cover third-party services or the planned open-source agent. The agent is not available; its data practices will be described before it is released.

Information processed

The website has no account registration or contact form. If you visit, Cloudflare Pages may process technical connection information, such as your IP address and request information, to deliver and protect the website. Thalia Agent does not use website analytics, advertising pixels or tracking tools.

If you email cybersec-star@proton.me, the operator receives your email address and any information or attachments you include. Please do not send confidential business, customer or other sensitive information.

Why information is used

Email is used to read and respond to your message. Technical connection information is processed to deliver and protect the website. Where applicable, this processing is based on the operator’s legitimate interests in communicating about the project and maintaining a secure website.

Service providers and international processing

The website is hosted by Cloudflare Pages. Contact email is handled through Proton Mail. These providers may process information as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy notices: Cloudflare Privacy Policy (https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) and Proton Privacy Policy (https://proton.me/legal/privacy). Cloudflare may process technical information outside Switzerland.

The operator does not sell email correspondence or use it for advertising.

Retention

Email correspondence is kept while needed to respond and handle the conversation, then deleted unless it must be retained to meet a legal obligation or resolve a dispute. Technical data handled by Cloudflare is subject to its applicable retention terms and settings.

Your requests

You can contact the operator at cybersec-star@proton.me with questions or requests concerning personal information in your correspondence. Requests will be handled under applicable data protection law.
