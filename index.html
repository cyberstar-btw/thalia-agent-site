An open-source project by cyberstar-btw

Bring unanswered proposals back into view.

Thalia Agent is a planned open-source assistant for small B2B teams. It will help find proposal conversations that may need follow-up, give a person the context to decide what comes next, and prepare a draft for them to review and send.

The agent is not built. This site explains the project, not a working product.

See the planned workflowView the project repository

Planned workflow

A prompt to review, not an automatic follow-up.

  1. 01

    Find a conversation

    Surface sent proposals that have no reply and may need another look.

  2. 02

    Choose what happens next

    Classify it as follow up now, revisit later, do not contact, lost, mistaken, or unsure.

  3. 03

    Prepare a draft

    Review suggested wording and decide yourself whether to send it.
